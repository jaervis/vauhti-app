FAQ

Answers before you ride.

How does the fixed price work?

You see the final price before you confirm the order. The price does not change during the ride, even if traffic slows down. Any surge is always shown in advance.

What if I need to cancel?

You can cancel free of charge until the driver has set off. After that a cancellation fee applies and is always shown before you confirm.

How is safety handled?

Every driver has a background check, a taxi driver licence and ongoing ratings. In the app you can share the trip, check the route and call emergency services with one tap.

Can I pay with employee benefits?

Yes. Vauhti supports the most common employee benefit cards and centralised invoicing. Receipts include a business ID and an itemised breakdown.

How do I apply to drive?

Send an application to kuljettajat@vauhti.fi. You need to be at least 21, hold a valid driving licence and a taxi driver licence, and pass a background check.

Where does Vauhti operate?

Vauhti currently operates in Helsinki, Tampere and Turku. Next we are expanding to Espoo, Vantaa and Oulu.
